Preger Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Seiji Matsunoshita ("the Operator") sets out the following policy on the information handled by the household budget app "Preger" ("the App").

1. Basic principles

  • The App has no Operator-run servers. The data you enter is stored only on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own iCloud.
  • The Operator has no means of reading the contents of your books, transactions, or photos.
  • The App does not use advertising, usage analytics tools, or tracking across other apps and websites.

2. Information the App handles

The App handles the following information.

  • Book data: transactions (date, amount, payee, memo, tags, etc.), accounts and categories, budgets, recurring transactions, and the names you enter for people.
  • Photos attached to transactions: photos such as receipts. A photo may show a store name, items purchased, part of a card number, and so on.
  • Imported files: the original file (such as a CSV) when you import one. It is kept together with the import record.
  • Settings: display and input settings.
  • Identifiers used to tell devices apart: the following two. Neither contains your name or the name of your device.
    • A random identifier created when you install the App. It is attached to each transaction as "the device that created it" and is used, after syncing, to recognize the same transaction entered on different devices. It changes if you reinstall the App.
    • A device identifier provided by Apple (the identifier for vendor). If you still hold a shared book after your plan has ended, it is used to choose the single device that keeps syncing.
  • Marker for who entered a transaction in a shared book: when a book is shared, transactions and other items carry a per-user iCloud number as "entered by". It is used to show the name of the person who entered an item on participants' screens.
  • Usage counts on the free plan: for example, the number of entries per month.
  • Purchase status: whether you have purchased a plan, as received from and verified with the App Store.

3. Where information is stored

  • Your device: all of the information above is stored on your device. A cache used to display widgets is also created on your device. Photos and imported files are not included in device backups. If you do not use iCloud sync, photos and imported files are not carried over when you restore a device from a backup or switch to a new device.
  • Your own iCloud (if you use sync): book data, photos, and imported files are stored in your own area of iCloud on Apple's servers and kept in step across devices signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Your own iCloud (even if you do not use sync): if you are signed in to iCloud, the usage counts on the free plan and the Apple device identifier above are placed in the small settings storage area of your own iCloud. This lets the counts and the rule for which device syncs carry over when you reinstall or use another device.
  • iCloud data is managed by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

4. Information sent outside your device

The App sends information outside your device only to the following two Apple services.

  • iCloud: for sync and book sharing.
  • App Store: for purchases, restoring purchases, and checking purchase status. Your name and payment information are handled by Apple and never reach the App or the Operator.

In addition, when you record by voice through Siri or Shortcuts, Apple performs the speech recognition. This follows your device's Siri settings.

5. Sharing books

  • When you share a book, the people you invite can view, and depending on their permissions edit, the book's transactions, photos, the names of people, and so on.
  • Invitations are sent through Apple's sharing mechanisms (Messages, Mail, etc.).
  • A participant's name is either the name set in that book or the name in Apple's sharing information, and is shown to the other participants as well.
  • When you stop sharing, participants can no longer see that book.

6. Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only when you photograph a receipt. Photos taken are not saved to the Photos app.
  • When you choose from the Photos app, only the photos you choose are loaded. The App does not ask for permission to access your whole photo library.
  • What is saved is a small black-and-white image with the edges cropped away. The original photo is not saved. Cropping is done on your device.

7. Exports and backups

When you export a CSV file or a backup file, it is saved to the location you choose (the Files app, another app you share to, and so on). How it is handled after that follows the rules of the service where you saved it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • When you delete a transaction: it disappears from the screen, and any photo attached to it is deleted at that moment. The deleted transaction remains as "deleted" and can be permanently erased from within the App. Data that you permanently erase is also automatically removed, after a while, from the sync records on your device.
  • When you delete the App: the data on your device is erased. Data in iCloud remains.
  • When your plan ends: the data in iCloud is kept, not deleted. If you purchase the plan again, syncing resumes where it left off.
  • To delete data in iCloud: on your iPhone, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Storage > Preger > Delete Data from iCloud. If you share a book, deleting it as the owner may also make it unavailable to participants.
  • The Operator does not hold your data, so the Operator cannot delete it or show you its contents.

9. Information that reaches the Operator from Apple

  • If "Share With App Developers" is turned on in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple sends the Operator crash records and usage information for the App. Apple states that it provides these in aggregated form or in a form that does not identify you. To stop sharing, turn "Share With App Developers" off.
  • For App Store sales, the Operator receives aggregate figures from Apple, such as counts by country. The Operator does not receive purchasers' names or similar details.

10. Laws in your region

The App does not collect your information on the Operator's side. For that reason, the rights granted by the law of the region where you live (such as accessing, correcting, or deleting your information) can be exercised by yourself, within the App and your iPhone settings. The App does not track you, so it behaves the same whether or not it receives a "Do Not Track" signal.

11. Children's use

The App can be used at any age. Because the App sends no information to the Operator, the Operator does not receive children's information.

12. Changes to this policy

When the contents change, this page will be updated and the date above revised. For significant changes, we will also announce them in the App's update notes.

13. Contact
