Preger Support

Support page for the household budget app "Preger".

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or requests, please send an email.

mattun.s@proton.me

When reporting a bug, it helps to include:

  • Your device (e.g. iPhone 16) and iOS version
  • The app version (shown at the bottom of Settings)
  • Which screen you were on and what you did when it happened

The App is made by one person, so a reply may take a few days. Your ledger data never reaches the Operator, so we cannot look at your data to answer questions.

Frequently asked questions

A plan I bought is not available

Tap "Restore Purchases" under "Purchases" in Settings. Purchases made with the same Apple Account will be restored.

I want to cancel a yearly plan

You can turn off auto-renewal in the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. If you turn it off at least 24 hours before the renewal date, you will not be charged for the next period.

Can my family use it too?

One-time and yearly plans support Apple Family Sharing, so family members can use the same plan. Tips are not included.

I want to move my data to a new device

If you turn on "iCloud Sync" in Settings, your ledgers will arrive on your new device signed in with the same Apple Account. If you do not use sync, export a file from "Backup & Restore" in Settings and import it on the new device.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device and in your own iCloud. It is never sent to the Operator's servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My data has disappeared

The Operator does not hold your data, so we cannot restore it. Please export your data regularly from "Backup & Restore". Receipt photos and imported files are not included in backups, so export them separately from "Data Management".
