Preger Disclaimer

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Seiji Matsunoshita ("the Operator") sets out the following notes on using the household budget app "Preger" ("the App").

1. Calculations and displayed figures

  • Balances, totals, budgets, analyses and charts are estimates based on the records you enter. Their accuracy and completeness are not guaranteed.
  • Please check the results of CSV imports and automatically created recurring transactions before relying on them.

2. Not professional advice

  • The App is a tool for recording and totaling your finances. It does not provide tax, accounting, legal or investment advice.
  • If you use it for tax filing or business bookkeeping, please verify the figures yourself or consult a professional.

3. Keeping your data

  • Your data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud. The Operator does not hold your data and cannot recover data that has been lost.
  • Data may be lost through device failure or loss, accidental deletion, iCloud problems and similar causes. Please export a backup of your books regularly using "Backup & Restore".
  • Receipt photos and imported files are included neither in your device backup nor in the App's backup. Please export them separately from "Saved Receipts" and "Imported Files" in "Data Management".

4. iCloud sync and shared books

  • Sync and sharing use Apple's services. Depending on the state of those services, they may be delayed or stop working.
  • Participants in a shared book can edit or delete its contents according to their permissions. Please use your own judgment when deciding whom to invite.

5. Changes to the App

  • Features may be changed, and the App may be discontinued.
